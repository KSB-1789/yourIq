# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Individuals seeking to evaluate their cognitive abilities (fluid intelligence, visual-spatial reasoning, quantitative induction, and pattern synthesis) without encountering subscription paywalls, forced sign-ups, or delayed results.

## Product Purpose

Provide an accessible, scientifically calibrated cognitive evaluation tool adhering to the Wechsler adult distribution scale ($\mu = 100, \sigma = 15$). Delivers immediate, transparent scoring, normative percentiles, question-by-question rule explanations, and local score history.

## Positioning

A zero-friction, fully client-side cognitive assessment that provides genuine psychometric evaluation with immediate unredacted results, zero advertising, and no subscription traps.

## Operating Context

Desktop and mobile modern web browsers. Evaluated across varying screen widths, from mobile phones to ultra-wide desktop monitors, requiring expansive, breathable layouts with balanced whitespace.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 20-question randomized assessment batteries dynamically selected from an expanded, difficulty-tiered psychometric bank.
- Randomized option ordering per question on every test session to prevent answer memorization on retests.
- Item Response Theory difficulty weighting and Gaussian Error Function ($\text{erf}$) percentile computation.
- Local storage assessment history allowing candidates to review and compare past attempts over time.
- High-definition printable Certificate of Performance with editable candidate name.
- 100% client-side execution with zero external data transmission.

## Product Principles

1. **Clarity and Restraint:** Communicate with academic confidence. Avoid promotional hype, competitor callouts, and artificial urgency.
2. **Expansive Breathing Room:** Avoid cramped, narrow containers on wide screens; use balanced grid systems and generous whitespace that respects wide displays.
3. **Impeccable Visual Hierarchy:** Strict typographic scale, crisp geometry, and high-contrast vector graphics.
4. **Immediate Transparency:** Scores, sub-domain breakdowns, and item-by-item solutions are unlocked immediately upon submission.
